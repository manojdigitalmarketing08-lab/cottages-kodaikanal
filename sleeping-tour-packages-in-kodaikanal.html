Suggestions to Improve Your Travel Experience

1

Book Early

Reserve your package in advance, especially for peak seasons (April–May, Dec–Jan).

2

Try Local Food

Don't miss Kodaikanal's homemade chocolates and hot soups at Coaker's Walk.

3

Carry a Camera

Capture stunning viewpoints like Dolphin's Nose, Moir Point and Berijam Lake.

4

Pack Layers

Weather shifts quickly in the hills — keep warm clothing handy even in summer.

5

Explore Hidden Spots

Ask your guide about lesser-known places like Poombarai Village or Mannavanur Lake.

6

Go Private for Couples

Choose a private cab for a more personalised, romantic experience.
