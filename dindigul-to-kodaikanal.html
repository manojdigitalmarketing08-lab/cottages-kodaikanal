Why most travellers choose a cab

Dindigul, a district headquarters well known for its locks and biryani, sits on the plains well before the hills begin. The road runs through Oddanchatram and Batlagundu before climbing a series of ghat bends into Kodaikanal, and a private cab handles all of it without a change of vehicle. You can leave straight from the railway station or bus stand, stop for a meal or a viewpoint photo whenever you like, and arrive at your cottage door rather than a bus stand you then need a second ride from.

The Distance

How Far Is Dindigul to Kodaikanal?

By road, Dindigul and Kodaikanal sit about 100 km apart, most of it flat highway running through Oddanchatram and Batlagundu before the road turns uphill. The final stretch is the ghat section — around 20 km of switchbacks that gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation in one go, which is why the last leg feels longer than the distance suggests.

On the Road

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

Typical road journey: 3–3.5 hours

A non-stop run in a private cab takes about 3 to 3.5 hours. Add a breakfast break near Oddanchatram or a photo stop on the ghat road and it stretches closer to 4 hours. Early-morning departures usually clear Dindigul town traffic fastest, while afternoon starts can run into slower ghat traffic during peak tourist season.

The Journey

Which Route Should You Take from Dindigul to Kodaikanal?

One road covers the whole trip, but it changes character three times — plains, foothills, then a mountain climb into Kodaikanal itself.

Start — Dindigul
Plain Roads · ~40 min

Dindigul to Oddanchatram

Open highway running west — the fastest, flattest stretch of the drive, and the easiest to make up time on if you start early.

Foothills · ~40 min

Oddanchatram to Batlagundu

The land starts to fold into hills here — a good stretch for a breakfast stop before the road narrows and starts climbing.

Mountain Road · ~90 min

The Kodaikanal Ghat Section

Roughly 20 km of hairpin bends climbing through forest, gaining most of Kodaikanal's elevation — this is where a driver who knows the road matters most.

Arrival

Kodaikanal Town

The ghat road opens out near Kodaikanal Lake, and your cab drops you directly at your cottage or hotel door.
