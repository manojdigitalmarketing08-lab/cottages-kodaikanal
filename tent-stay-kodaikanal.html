Planning Your Kodaikanal Tent Stay

Is a Tent Stay Right for Your Trip?

A tent stay works best as a one or two-night add-on, not your whole trip's base — facilities are simpler than a cottage or hotel, and nights get genuinely cold. Adventure travelers, college groups and couples looking for a novelty night tend to enjoy it most. Families with very young kids or anyone needing full privacy usually do better with a cottage.

Tent Stay vs Cottage in Kodaikanal: Which Fits Your Group

Cottages give you a private, weatherproof space with a kitchen; tent stays trade that for an open-sky campfire experience. Most guests combine both — a cottage for most nights, one tent night for the experience. If your whole group wants an outdoor night together, ask about a full-site buyout.

Browse tent stay sites →

Best Time to Book a Tent Stay, Season by Season

December to February brings the clearest skies for campfires and stargazing, but also the coldest nights — pack accordingly. Monsoon months (July to September) aren't ideal for camping given the rain. April to June is a milder middle ground if you want warmer nights without the monsoon risk.

Nights can drop to single digits (Celsius), especially December to February. Our tents come with proper bedding, but pack warm layers regardless — it's genuinely cold after dark.

It varies by site — some tent stays have attached bathrooms, others use a shared block nearby. Tell us your preference when you enquire and we'll confirm which sites match.

04 Can families with young children do a tent stay?

Yes, though we usually suggest it as a one-night add-on rather than the whole trip's base, given the cold nights and simpler facilities compared to a cottage.

Most guests treat it as a one or two-night experience rather than a full-trip base, since facilities are simpler than a cottage or hotel. Combine it with another stay if your trip runs longer.
