The Route

Three days, one slow loop

The lake first, a full day for the forest, falls and valley, and a gentle last morning before drop-off. Pick a day to see its stops.

Day 1 · Arrival, check-in & lake sightseeing

  1. 12:00 PM

    Arrival & pickup

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, bus stand or railway station.

  2. 01:00 PM

    Hotel check-in

    Settle into your room and freshen up before heading out.

  3. 02:30 PM

    Kodaikanal Lake & Bryant Park

    Boat on the star-shaped lake, then walk through Bryant Park nearby.

  4. 04:15 PM

    Coaker’s Walk, Upper Lake View & Green Valley View

    A cliffside path linking three of the best panoramic viewpoints.

  5. 08:00 PM

    Dinner & overnight stay Included

    Back at the hotel for dinner and rest.

Day 2 · Forest, falls & valley

  1. 08:00 AM

    Breakfast Included

    Breakfast at the hotel before a full day of sightseeing.

  2. 09:00 AM

    Pillar Rocks, Pine Forest & Guna Cave

    Three granite pillars, a shaded pine trail and the cave made famous by the film “Guna.”

  3. 11:00 AM

    Moir Point & Pambar Falls

    A quieter ridge-top viewpoint, then a roadside waterfall stop.

  4. 01:00 PM

    Lunch break On your own

    A short stop for lunch. It is not part of the package.

  5. 02:00 PM

    Silver Cascade Falls

    A roadside waterfall on the descent from Kodaikanal, popular for photos.

  6. 03:00 PM

    Vattakanal & Dolphin’s Nose

    A quiet village trail leading to the Dolphin’s Nose viewpoint over the valley.

  7. 07:30 PM

    Dinner & overnight stay Included

    Back at the hotel for dinner and rest.

Day 3 · Optional Berijam Lake & departure

  1. 07:30 AM

    Breakfast Included

    Breakfast at the hotel before the final morning out.

  2. 08:30 AM

    Berijam Lake* Permit

    A pristine, restricted-access lake inside reserved forest land.

  3. 10:30 AM

    Rose Garden

    A last walk through Rose Garden before wrapping up the trip.

  4. 12:00 PM

    Checkout & drop-off

    Check out and get dropped at your pickup point.

*Berijam Lake needs a forest department permit with limited daily entries. We confirm availability for your dates before adding it to the plan.

How many nights of stay are included?

Two nights across the three-day itinerary. The room category depends on the tier you choose.

Is a permit required for Berijam Lake?

Yes. Berijam Lake is inside reserved forest land and needs a forest department permit with limited daily entries. We check availability for your dates before including it.

Are lunch and entrance tickets included?

No. Lunch and attraction entrance tickets are paid on the day unless your confirmed package says otherwise.

Is the cab private?

Yes. The package is built around a private cab for all three days. Confirm the vehicle type and seating capacity when you book.

Can the itinerary be changed?

Yes, where the confirmed terms allow it. The order can also shift with weather, traffic and attraction timings.

Can I be picked up from Madurai, Coimbatore or Palani?

Yes. Pickup is available from Coimbatore, Madurai, Dindigul, Palani and Kodai Road, and from your hotel, bus stand or railway station in Kodaikanal.

How fast will I get a price?

We check real availability for your dates and group size, and usually reply within a few hours.
