The Route

3 Day / 2 Nights Tour Experience

A more relaxed pace than our 1-day whirlwind route: arrival and lakeside sightseeing on day one, a full day covering the forest, viewpoint and waterfall loop on day two, and a slower final morning before drop-off on day three.

Day 1 · Arrival, Check-in & Lake Sightseeing

5 Stops
12:00 PM

Arrival & Pickup

Our driver picks you up from your hotel, bus stand or railway station.

01:00 PM

Hotel Check-in

Settle into your room and freshen up before heading out.

02:30 PM

Kodaikanal Lake & Bryant Park

Boating on the star-shaped lake, followed by a walk through the manicured Bryant Park nearby.

04:15 PM

Coaker's Walk, Upper Lake View & Green Valley View

A cliffside walking path linking three of Kodaikanal's best panoramic viewpoints.

08:00 PM

Dinner & Overnight Stay

Return to the hotel for dinner and rest for the night.

Day 2 · Full Sightseeing Day

6 Stops
08:00 AM

Breakfast

Breakfast at the hotel before setting out for a full day of sightseeing.

09:00 AM

Pillar Rocks, Pine Forest & Guna Cave

Three granite pillars, a shaded pine forest trail and the cave made famous by the film “Guna.”

11:00 AM

Moir Point & Pambar Falls

A quieter ridge-top viewpoint followed by a roadside waterfall stop.

01:00 PM

Lunch Break

A short stop for lunch on your own (not included in the package).

02:00 PM

Silver Cascade Falls

A roadside waterfall stop on the descent from Kodaikanal, popular for photos.

03:00 PM

Vattakanal & Dolphin's Nose

A quieter village trail leading to the Dolphin's Nose viewpoint over the valley.

07:30 PM

Dinner & Overnight Stay

Return to the hotel for dinner and rest for the night.

Checkout
07:30 AM

Breakfast

Berijam Lake*

A pristine, restricted-access lake inside reserved forest land (permit required, subject to availability).

10:30 AM

Rose Garden

A last walk through Rose Garden before wrapping up the trip.

12:00 PM

Checkout & Drop-off

Checkout from the hotel and drop-off at your pickup point.
