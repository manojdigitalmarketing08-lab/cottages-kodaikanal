Dormitory vs Budget Cottage: Which Costs Less

A dormitory bed is almost always the cheapest option we list, undercutting even our lowest-priced budget cottage. The trade-off is privacy — dorms are shared rooms with lockers, cottages are private units with their own bathroom. If cost is the deciding factor and privacy isn't, a dorm bed wins.

Best Time to Book a Dormitory Bed, Season by Season

April to June and December are Kodaikanal's busiest stretches, and dormitory beds — being the cheapest listing — go first. Monsoon months (July to September) are far easier to book on short notice. If your dates are flexible, a weekday stay outside peak season means faster confirmation.
