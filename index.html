What Is a Cottage in Kodaikanal?

A cottage here is a private, standalone building — your own front door, not a hotel room.

  • Private, standalone building — your own front door
  • Usually comes with a small kitchen and a private sit-out
  • No shared host, restaurant, or lawns to work around
  • Fenced private garden, separate from the property next door
  • Sizes range from a single room to multi-bedroom group properties
