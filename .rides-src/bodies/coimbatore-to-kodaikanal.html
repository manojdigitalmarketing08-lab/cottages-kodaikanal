The long haul, leg by leg

Which Route Should You Take from Coimbatore to Kodaikanal?

One road covers the whole trip, but it changes character three times: plains, foothills, then a mountain climb.

  1. ~90 min

    Coimbatore to Palani

    Open highway southeast through Palladam, Tiruppur and Dindigul. The fastest, flattest stretch; easy to make up time if you start early.

  2. ~45 min

    Palani to the Ghat Base

    The land folds into the Palani Hills. A good stretch for a breakfast stop before the road narrows.

  3. ~90 min

    The Kodaikanal Ghat Section

    About 20 km of hairpin bends through forest. This is where a driver who knows the road matters most.

  4. Arrival

    Kodaikanal Town

    The ghat road opens out at Kodaikanal Lake, worth a short stop before you're dropped at your cottage or hotel door.

How Far Is Coimbatore to Kodaikanal?

About 180 km, most of it flat highway to Palani. The last 20 km of switchbacks gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation, which is why the final leg feels longer than it looks.

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

4 to 5 hours non-stop; closer to 6 with a breakfast break near Palani or photo stops. Early departures clear Coimbatore traffic fastest.
