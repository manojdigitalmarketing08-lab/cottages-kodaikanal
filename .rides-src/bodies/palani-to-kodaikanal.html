Which Route Should You Take from Palani to Kodaikanal?

The drive runs mainly along SH 83 and NH 183, flat near Oddanchatram, then a climb through forest and tea plantations. An alternative stretch on the same highway network is used if the ghat sees temporary closures.

~30minutes

Palani to Oddanchatram

A short, open stretch out of the temple town. Quick if you leave straight after darshan.

~20minutes

Oddanchatram to the Ghat Base

The land folds into hills: a good spot for a quick break before the climb.

~70minutes

The Kodaikanal Ghat Section

About 20 km of hairpins through dense forest and tea plantations, with viewpoints opening at several bends. Most of the elevation is gained here.

How Far Is Palani to Kodaikanal?

About 65 km, one of the shorter gateway routes. The last 20 km of switchbacks gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation.

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

2 to 2.5 hours non-stop; the drive rarely goes past 3 hours. Early departures after darshan usually clear traffic fastest.
