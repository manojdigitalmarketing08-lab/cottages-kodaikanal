The distance

How Far Is Madurai to Kodaikanal?

About 120 km by road, most of it flat highway through Dindigul, Oddanchatram and Batlagundu before the road turns uphill into the Palani Hills. The final stretch is the ghat section: around 20 km of switchbacks that gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation in one go, which is why the last leg feels longer than the distance suggests.

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

A non-stop run takes 3.5 to 4 hours. Add a breakfast break near Dindigul or a photo stop on the ghat road and it's closer to 5 hours. Early-morning flights or trains usually mean the cleanest run up, before afternoon traffic builds in peak season.
