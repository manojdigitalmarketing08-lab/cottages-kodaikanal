The drive

Which Route Should You Take from Dindigul to Kodaikanal?

One road, three characters: plains, foothills, then the mountain climb.

  • ~40 min

    Dindigul to Oddanchatram

    Open highway running west. The fastest, flattest stretch.

  • ~40 min

    Oddanchatram to Batlagundu

    The land folds into hills. A good stretch for breakfast before the climb.

  • ~90 min

    The Kodaikanal Ghat Section

    Roughly 20 km of hairpin bends through forest, where a driver who knows the road matters most.

  • Arrival

    Kodaikanal Town

    The ghat road opens out near the lake, and your cab drops you at your door.

How Far Is Dindigul to Kodaikanal?

About 100 km, mostly flat through Oddanchatram and Batlagundu. The last 20 km of switchbacks gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation.

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

3 to 3.5 hours non-stop, about 4 with a breakfast stop near Oddanchatram. Early starts clear town traffic fastest.
