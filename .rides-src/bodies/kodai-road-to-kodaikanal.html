Down the line

Which Route Should You Take from Kodai Road to Kodaikanal?

One road, two characters: flat plains near the station, then a mountain climb.

  1. ~25 min

    Station to Batlagundu

    A short, flat run. Easy to make up a few minutes if your train is late.

  2. ~30 min

    Batlagundu to Ghat Base

    Foothills, and a good breakfast stop before the climb.

  3. ~75 min

    The Ghat Section

    About 20 km of hairpins through forest, where the driver's local knowledge counts.

  4. Arrival

    Kodaikanal Town

    Out near the lake and on to your door.

How Far Is Kodai Road to Kodaikanal?

About 80 km, mostly flat through Batlagundu. The last 20 km of switchbacks gain most of Kodaikanal's 2,100 metre elevation, so it feels longer than it is.

How Much Time Does the Journey Take?

2.5 to 3 hours non-stop; about 3.5 with a breakfast break near Batlagundu. Early-morning trains mean the cleanest run up the ghat.
