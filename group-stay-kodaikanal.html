Group Stay vs Resort in Kodaikanal: Which Fits Your Trip

Group stay cottages feel more personal — connected rooms and a shared common area, closer to a big house than a hotel. Resorts bundle dining halls, lawns and event space, better suited to weddings or formal functions. If your trip is mostly about the group hanging out together rather than hosting an event, a group stay cottage usually fits better.

Best Time to Book a Group Stay, Season by Season

December and April to June are peak season for college trips and family reunions, when the biggest group properties book out first. Monsoon months (July to September) have more availability and better rates, though outdoor group activities become weather-dependent. Booking 3 to 4 weeks ahead gives the most flexibility either way.
