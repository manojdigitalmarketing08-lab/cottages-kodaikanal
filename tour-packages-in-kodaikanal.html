What Will You Experience Each Day and Night?

A look at what each day and night of a Kodaikanal trip feels like.

Your Days

Day 1

Arrival & Lake Sightseeing

  • Pickup and hotel check-in
  • Kodaikanal Lake & Bryant Park
  • Coaker's Walk and valley viewpoints
Day 2

Full Sightseeing Day

  • Pillar Rocks, Pine Forest & Guna Cave
  • Moir Point & Pambar Falls
  • Silver Cascade, Vattakanal & Dolphin's Nose
Day 3

Slow Morning & Departure

  • Berijam Lake* (permit, subject to availability)
  • Rose Garden walk
  • Checkout and drop-off

Your Nights

Night 1

Dinner & First Night in the Hills

  • Return to your stay after sightseeing
  • Dinner at the hotel
  • Cool hill air and a quiet first night
Night 2

Rest After a Full Day

  • Dinner after a full sightseeing day
  • Comfortable room or private villa
  • Breakfast before the next morning
Night 3

A Slower Last Evening

  • For 3-night trips
  • Relaxed evening at your stay
  • Bonfire or candlelight dinner can be added on request

Stops and timings follow our 1, 2 and 3 day packages and are confirmed against travel time, weather and attraction timings. *Berijam Lake needs a forest permit with limited daily entries.

What Should You Know Before Booking?

1

Book early

Peak seasons are April–May and December–January.

2

Pack layers

Weather shifts quickly in the hills, even in summer.

3

Carry a camera

Dolphin's Nose, Moir Point and Coaker's Walk reward it.

4

Allow for the ghat road

The drive up takes time; plan to arrive before evening.

5

Try local food

Homemade chocolates and hot soups at Coaker's Walk.

6

Share your interests

Nature, adventure or a quiet break: we tune the route.
