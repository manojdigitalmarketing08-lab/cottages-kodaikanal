Resorts vs Cottages in Kodaikanal: Which Fits Your Group

Resorts give you shared dining, lawns and event space, built for groups of 30 and up who need to gather in one place. Cottages suit families of 4 to 8 who want a private house with a kitchen. If your trip includes a function, ceremony or group meal everyone attends together, a resort is almost always the better fit.

Best Time to Book a Resort in Kodaikanal, Season by Season

December and April to June are Kodaikanal's wedding-season peaks, when resort lawns book out weeks ahead. Monsoon months (July to September) bring lower demand and better negotiating room on rates, though outdoor lawn events become weather-dependent. If your date is flexible, a weekday booking outside peak season is the fastest to confirm.

02 What's the difference between a resort and a cottage in Kodaikanal?

Cottages are private, self-contained units, usually with a kitchen. Resorts bundle multiple rooms with shared facilities — dining halls, lawns, sometimes a pool — built around groups and events rather than one family cooking for itself.

03 Can resorts handle large group bookings for reunions or offsites?

Yes — several of our listed resorts scale from 50 up to 200 guests across connected rooms and shared common spaces, which is usually why groups pick a resort over splitting across separate cottages.

04 Do any Kodaikanal resorts have a swimming pool?

A handful of our listed resorts have pool access, though it varies by season and property. Mention it when you enquire and we'll confirm which resorts have it open for your dates.

05 Is food included when booking a resort in Kodaikanal?

Most resorts run an on-site dining hall with meals included in the package, though the exact inclusions vary by property. We'll confirm what's covered before you lock in your quote.

06 Can resorts arrange bonfires or outdoor evening events?

Many of our resort listings have lawn space set up for bonfires and outdoor games in the evening — a common ask for reunions and college trips. Tell us when you enquire and we'll flag which properties support it.

07 How far in advance should I book a resort for a large event?
